Both phones need the same carrier. Put them 1–3 m apart, facing up, media volume high. Exchange keys once, check the verify codes match, then send. Throughput is 64 baud — key exchange takes ~9 s, a short message ~6 s.
iOS: must be served over https, and the silent switch must be off.
If anyone can hear it: raise the carrier before raising the level — distortion from a hard-driven speaker is audible at any frequency.
